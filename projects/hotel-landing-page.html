Welcome to Cape Breeze

Experience Cape Coast in comfort.

A peaceful boutique stay inspired by the coast.

Project overview

A digital welcome shaped around place and comfort.

Cape Breeze was created as a fictional boutique hotel concept that communicates the calm, character and hospitality associated with Ghana’s coast.

The landing page introduces the hotel, presents three room options, explains available amenities and highlights nearby Cape Coast destinations.

The experience ends with a structured booking enquiry form that allows prospective guests to provide their dates, room preference and contact information.

The challenge

Creating an inviting hotel experience while keeping the path to enquiry clear.

Problem

Balancing atmosphere and useful information

A hospitality website needs strong imagery and emotion, but visitors must still understand room choices, amenities, location and the next action.

Solution

Structured visual storytelling

Clear sections guide visitors from the hotel introduction through rooms and local attractions before presenting the booking-enquiry form.

Key functionality

A focused experience from discovery to enquiry.

01

Responsive navigation

Desktop and mobile navigation with accessible menu controls, Escape-key support and responsive behaviour.

02

Room presentation

Three room types display starting prices, capacity, bed type, views and short descriptions.

03

Amenities overview

Pool, dining, Wi-Fi, transport, reception and business facilities are presented clearly.

04

Destination content

Cape Coast Castle, Kakum National Park, Elmina and local beaches connect the hotel concept to its location.

05

Enquiry form

Guests can provide contact details, dates, guest count, room preference and an optional message.

06

Production monitoring

Cloudflare analytics and uptime monitoring help confirm that the deployed website remains available.

Technical approach

Lightweight technology with production details.

Structure

Semantic HTML organises the story, rooms, amenities, gallery, testimonials and contact information.

Styling

Responsive CSS creates the coastal visual identity and adapts the complete page across screen sizes.

Behaviour

JavaScript manages mobile navigation and interaction states while preserving accessible attributes.

Forms

Formspree processes booking enquiries without requiring a custom application backend.

Hosting

Cloudflare publishes the website and provides analytics, with UptimeRobot monitoring its availability.

Outcome

A complete hospitality concept available as a live, responsive website.

The finished project demonstrates how a static website can still provide a polished brand experience, meaningful interactions and a working enquiry pathway.

It strengthened my experience with responsive layouts, accessible navigation, third-party form integration, deployment, analytics and production monitoring.
